Why this for a Coptic church

Abouna is made from inside the church. Andrew Ishak grew up in the Coptic churches of Northern California, and the series comes from countless hours interviewing, observing and reading about the Coptic experience. It is no one priest's story. It is a story meant to resonate with anyone who has grown up in an ethno-religious community.

I'm not writing this from outside of the church. I am somebody who loves the church. I've been a part of church my whole life. I brought my wife to the church. I'm bringing my kids to church.

Father Beemen Kolta is a newly ordained priest doing his best to serve his people while feeling like he is in over his head. The series shows the priests as God-fearing and imperfect, and because of that they can relate to the people. Just as God took human form, the priest also is human.

Something that is not disrespectful at church, that is fun for people, that helps them connect with the church long-term. You build a strong church by building a strong community.

The first episode screened at ten festivals and numerous churches across three continents, and took home Best Feature at the Beacon of Light and Mosaic Film Festivals. Members of all faiths and those outside of a faith community are very welcome.

The evening

Episode Two runs about 65 minutes. After the film, a Q&A with Andrew Ishak and a priest from your church, who gives the church's perspective on what the film shows.

What the church provides

  • 1A hall or theater with a projector and sound. Tell us what you know about your A/V setup, or how you would like to show the film.
  • 2A date. Days of the week when you already have an established meeting time work best.
  • 3A priest for the Q&A.
  • 4Andrew's travel for your stop. On a run of two or three churches, each covers its share, not a full flight.

Who it is for

13 and up

Members of all faiths and those outside of a faith community are very welcome.

The money

The screening is free or a suggested donation at the door, your choice, and the church keeps all of it. The church covers Andrew's travel for its stop. The evening closes with a 60-second ask: a QR code to a support page for Episode 3.

Sign-ups

Everyone signs up in advance through a link on watchabouna.com, even when the screening is free. Andrew hosts the sign-ups and shares a link with you that shows the names and the count, nothing else, so you know a week out how many are coming.
