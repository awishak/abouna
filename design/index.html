Three ways watchabouna.com could look

Each one is a working page: the real video, the real stills, the real words, at phone and laptop size. Every option has a Home and a Screenings page; the bar at the top switches between them and between options. Nothing here is live on the site.

What is the same in all three: the video stays; the three easy links sit right under the hero; the screenings page is a map, then the list, then Request a screening; no text under 15px, most of it 17 to 20. Screenings marked "example" are made up to show the shape. Pick one, or name the pieces you want from each, on the tracker (question 75).

  • Fonts: A keeps Cormorant Garamond for titles. B is Outfit only. C uses Bebas Neue for titles.
  • Maps use the same free tiles and library as the vacation planner.
  • The map pins and the list rows would come from the screenings database once built, so adding a screening in admin puts it on both.
